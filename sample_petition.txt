MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. The petitioner states that an electricity bill dated 05.03.2018 was issued by Respondent No. 2 alleging outstanding dues.

2. The petitioner did not challenge the said bill at the relevant time and has approached this Hon’ble Court after a delay of more than six years without providing any explanation for such delay.

3. The petitioner has not annexed any payment receipts, meter readings, inspection reports, or documentary material in support of his claim.

4. The petitioner admits that an alternative statutory remedy of appeal before the Electricity Grievance Redressal Forum is available, however the same has not been exhausted.

5. The petitioner has directly invoked the writ jurisdiction of this Hon’ble Court without approaching the competent authority.

6. The petitioner merely alleges that the bill is excessive and arbitrary, but no technical or factual basis has been provided.

7. The recovery notice has been challenged without placing any material on record to establish illegality.

PRAYER

In view of the above, the petitioner prays that this Hon’ble Court may be pleased to:

a) Quash the electricity bill dated 05.03.2018;

b) Stay the recovery proceedings;

c) Pass any other order as this Hon’ble Court may deem fit.

AND FOR THIS ACT OF KINDNESS THE PETITIONER SHALL EVER PRAY.
